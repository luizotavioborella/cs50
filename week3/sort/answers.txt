sort1 uses: Bubble sort

How do you know?: It is much faster when the numbers are already sorted, but takes much longer with random or reversed numbers.

sort2 uses: Merge sort

How do you know?: It has similar running times with random, sorted, and reversed numbers, even with 50000 numbers.

sort3 uses: Selection sort

How do you know?: It takes a similar amount of time with random and sorted numbers because it always searches for the smallest element in the unsorted portion.
